Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

This website is operated by Dr. A Sliwinska, MD. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains what information is collected through this site and how it is used.

Information we collect

The only personal information collected through this website is your email address, which you may voluntarily provide through the newsletter subscription form.

No other personal data is collected. This site does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

How your email is used

Your email address is used solely to send you the newsletter — periodic updates with articles, insights, and content published on this site. Your email will never be sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than delivering the newsletter.

Newsletter service

The newsletter is delivered through Buttondown, a third-party email service. When you subscribe, your email address is stored and processed by Buttondown in accordance with their privacy policy. Buttondown may collect basic analytics such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, in order to help improve the newsletter.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included at the bottom of every email. Upon unsubscribing, your email address will be removed from the mailing list.

Data retention

Your email address is retained only for as long as you remain subscribed to the newsletter. Once you unsubscribe, your data is deleted from the mailing list.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please reach out via the contact information provided on the site.
